# OSCALink: A Web-Based Portal for Senior Citizen Monitoring

**Title**: OSCALink: A Web-Based Portal for Senior Citizen Monitoring, Records Management, Assistance Request, and Appointment Scheduling System for Cotabato City

**Authors**: Christopher Andre H. Valencia, Prince Rashid A. Vios, Nice Collin G. Uko, Daisy N. Wacan
**Date**: March 2026

---

## 1. Introduction
Senior citizens require continuous monitoring and proper support to ensure their health, safety, and overall well-being. In Cotabato City, the Office for Senior Citizen Affairs (OSCA) is responsible for maintaining records and providing assistance to elderly residents. Currently, many records are managed manually (paper/spreadsheets), leading to disorganized data, risk of loss, and inefficient service delivery.

## 2. Statement of the Problem
The Office for Senior Citizen Affairs (OSCA) faces challenges due to manual processes:
1. **Difficulty in Monitoring**: Identifying active vs. deceased residents.
2. **Slow Processing**: Manual verification delays assistance (medical, financial, burial).
3. **Lack of Scheduling**: Appointments for checkups and registrations are unorganized.
4. **Risk of Data Loss**: Paper-based records are prone to physical damage or misplacement.

## 3. Objectives

### General Objective
To develop **OSCALink**, a web-based system that enabling OSCA to monitor senior citizens, manage records, process assistance requests, and schedule appointments across barangays in Cotabato City.

### Specific Objectives
- **Registry**: Develop a feature for digital registration and management of records.
- **Monitoring**: Implement a status system (Active, Inactive, Transferred, Deceased).
- **Assistance**: Enable submission and processing for medical, financial, and assistive devices.
- **Scheduling**: Create an appointment system for municipal services.
- **Reporting**: Generate organized analytics for city officials.

## 4. Scope and Limitations

### Scope
- Monitoring and management of senior citizen data in Cotabato City.
- Processing of support requests (Financial, Medical, etc.).
- Appointment scheduling module.
- Role-based access for OSCA staff and Barangay officials.

### Limitations
- restricted to senior citizens in Cotabato City.
- Restricted to authorized users only.
- Requires internet access for real-time synchronization.
