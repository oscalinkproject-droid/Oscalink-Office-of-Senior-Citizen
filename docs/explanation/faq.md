# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is OSCALink?
OSCALink is a web-based portal for the Office for Senior Citizen Affairs (OSCA) in Cotabato City. It helps manage senior citizen records, assistance requests, and appointments.

### Who can use OSCALink?
- **City Administrators** — Full access to all features
- **Barangay Officials** — Access to their assigned barangay's data

### What browsers are supported?
- Chrome (recommended)
- Firefox
- Microsoft Edge
- Safari

## Accounts & Login

### How do I get login credentials?
Contact your system administrator to create an account.

### I forgot my password. What should I?
Contact your administrator to reset your password.

### Can I share my account with others?
No. Each user should have their own account for security and accountability.

## Senior Records

### What information is stored for each senior?
- Name, age, birthdate
- Address and contact number
- Barangay
- Status (Active, Inactive, Transferred, Deceased)
- Emergency contact
- Document scans

### How do I register a new senior?
1. Go to Directory page
2. Click "Register New Senior"
3. Fill in the required information
4. Submit the form

### What do the status values mean?
See [Understanding Senior Statuses](./statuses.md) for details.

## Assistance Requests

### What types of assistance are available?
- Medical Support
- Financial Aid
- Burial Assistance
- Assistive Devices

### How do I process a request?
1. Go to Requests page
2. Filter by category
3. Click on a request
4. Update the status
5. The applicant will receive an email notification

## Reports

### How do I generate a report?
1. Go to Reports page (Admin only)
2. Select report type
3. Set filters (date, barangay)
4. Click Export PDF or CSV

### What report formats are available?
- **PDF** — For printing and formal documents
- **CSV** — For data analysis in spreadsheet software

## Technical

### Why is the page not loading?
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the browser
- Clear browser cache

### Who do I contact for technical issues?
Contact your system administrator or IT support team.
