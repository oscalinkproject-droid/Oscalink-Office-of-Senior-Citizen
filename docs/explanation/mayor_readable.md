# OSCALink Roles Explained

## For Monkeys with Brains: Understanding Who Does What

This guide explains the three main roles in OSCALink and why each role exists. Think of it like a football team — everyone has a position, and everyone matters.

---

## Quick Comparison

| Role | Head (Coach) | MSWD (Assistant Coach) | Barangay Official (Player) | Mayor (Team Owner) |
|:----|:-------------:|:----------------------:|:-------------------------:|:-------------------:|
| **Can View All** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ❌ Own barangay only | ✅ Yes |
| **Can Process Requests** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| **Can Register Seniors** | ✅ Yes | ❌ No (Verifies & Validates only) | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| **Can Edit Records** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| **Can Generate Reports** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ❌ Own barangay only | ✅ Yes |
| **Can Manage Users** | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No |
| **Final Approval Power** | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No |

## 1. Mayor — The Team Owner 👔

### What They Do
The mayor is like the team owner — they don't play the game, but they need to know what's happening. They oversee the entire city government.

### Key Responsibilities
- **Executive Oversight** — Watch everything from above
- **Certify Reports** — Sign official documents for DSWD and City Council
- **Budget Approval** — Authorize funds for assistance programs
- **Accountability** — Answer to higher government (DSWD, Congress)

### What They CAN Do in OSCALink
- 📊 View city-wide dashboard
- 👴 View all senior citizens
- 📝 View all assistance requests
- 📄 Generate & export reports (PDF/CSV)
- 📅 View appointment calendar

### What They CANNOT Do
- ❌ Register new seniors
- ❌ Process assistance requests
- ❌ Edit any records
- ❌ Manage user accounts

### Law/Rule Reference
> **RA 7432** — Senior Citizens Act of 1992
> Mandates mayors to establish and maintain OSCA offices.
> 
> **Memorandum Circular 2005-03** — Requires OSCA reporting to DSWD and Congress. Mayor signs as chief executive.

---

## 2. MSWD Officer — The Assistant Coach 🎯

### What They Do
MSWD stands for **Municipal/City Social Welfare and Development**. This officer is like an assistant coach — they help the OSCA Head run the program day-to-day.

### Key Responsibilities
- **Verify Senior Records** — Make sure documents are real
- **Process Assistance** — Handle aid requests (medical, financial, burial, devices)
- **Liaison Work** — Connect seniors to DSWD programs
- **PhilHealth Processing** — Handle health insurance matters

### What They CAN Do in OSCALink
- 📊 View city-wide dashboard
- 👴 View all senior citizens (city-wide)
- ✅ Verify senior documents (check authenticity)
- ✅ Validate senior records (confirm details)
- 📝 Edit senior records (city-wide)
- 📄 Generate reports & PhilHealth exports
- 📅 Create/manage appointments

### What They CANNOT Do
- ❌ Register new seniors (Barangay Officials do this)
- ❌ Final approval (only OSCA Head can do this)
- ❌ Manage user accounts
- ❌ Access system settings

### Law/Rule Reference
> **DILG MC 2005-63** — MSWD serves as Liaison Officer between OSCA and DSWD. Function is to verify and validate documents, NOT to register new seniors.
> 
> **RA 9257** — Senior Citizens Act (amended RA 7432) — Mandates MSWD Officers to assist in social welfare programs and verify beneficiary eligibility.

---

## 3. Barangay Official — The Player On The Field 🏃

### What They Do
Barangay Officials are the front-line players. They work directly with seniors in their specific barangay (neighborhood/village).

### Key Responsibilities
- **Local Verification** — Know the seniors in their area
- **Register New Seniors** — Add residents to the system
- **Process Local Requests** — Handle assistance for their barangay
- **Coordinate Events** — Schedule vaccinations, medical missions

### What They CAN Do in OSCALink
- 📊 View dashboard (own barangay only)
- 👴 View senior citizens (own barangay only)
- ✅ Process assistance requests (own barangay only)
- 📝 Register & edit seniors (own barangay only)
- 📅 Create/manage appointments (own barangay)
- 📄 Generate reports (own barangay only)

### What They CANNOT Do
- ❌ View data from other barangays
- ❌ Process requests outside their barangay
- ❌ Generate city-wide reports
- ❌ Manage user accounts

### Law/Rule Reference
> **RA 7432** — Senior Citizens Act of 1992
> Barangays are the first point of contact for senior citizens.
> 
> **Local Ordinance** — Each barangay must maintain updated lists of senior residents.
