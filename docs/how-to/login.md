# How to Log In

Quick steps to log into OSCALink.

## Steps

1. **Open the browser** and go to the OSCALink URL
2. **Click "Access Administrative Portal"** on the landing page
3. **Enter your email** in the login field
4. **Enter your password**
5. **Click "Sign In"**

You will be redirected to your dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "Invalid credentials" error | Check your email and password. Contact admin if forgotten. |
| Page not loading | Check your internet connection. |
| Redirected back to login | Clear browser cookies and try again. |

## Security Note

- Never share your password
- Always log out when finished
- Contact admin if you suspect unauthorized access
