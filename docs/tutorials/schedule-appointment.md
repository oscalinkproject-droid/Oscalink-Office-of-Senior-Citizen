# Scheduling an Appointment

This tutorial will guide you through scheduling and managing appointments for senior citizens.

## Objectives

By the end of this tutorial, you will be able to:
- View the appointment calendar
- Schedule new appointments
- Manage (reschedule or cancel) existing appointments

## Step 1: Navigate to Timeline

1. From the sidebar, click **"Timeline"**
2. You will see the appointment calendar view
3. Click **"Appointments"** tab for a detailed list

## Step 2: Understand the Calendar View

The calendar displays appointments in two formats:

- **Calendar View** — Visual calendar showing appointments by date
- **List View** — Text-based list of upcoming appointments

### Calendar Colors

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Blue | Health Checkup |
| Green | Registration |
| Yellow | Document Processing |
| Red | Urgent/Critical |

## Step 3: Schedule a New Appointment

1. Click the **"New Appointment"** or **"+"** button
2. Fill in the appointment form:

### Required Information

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Senior** | Select the senior citizen | Juan dela Cruz |
| **Service Type** | Type of appointment | Health Checkup |
| **Date** | Appointment date | 2026-04-15 |
| **Time** | Appointment time | 09:00 AM |
| **Notes** | Additional details | Bring medical records |

### Service Types

- **Health Checkup** — Regular medical examination
- **Registration** — New senior registration
- **Document Processing** — ID renewal, benefits enrollment
- **Benefits Assessment** — Evaluating assistance eligibility
- **Follow-up** — Post-assistance follow-up

## Step 4: View Appointment Details

Click on any appointment to see:
- Senior citizen's full information
- Contact numbers
- Appointment history
- Status

## Step 5: Manage Appointments

### Reschedule

1. Click on the appointment
2. Click **"Reschedule"**
3. Select new date and time
4. Confirm the change

### Cancel

1. Click on the appointment
2. Click **"Cancel"**
3. Provide reason for cancellation
4. The appointment will be marked as cancelled

---

## Step 6: Today's Schedule

The Dashboard shows a **"Today's Schedule"** widget with:
- Appointments scheduled for today
- Senior names and service types
- Quick contact information

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Can't find a senior in the appointment form
- **Solution:** Ensure the senior is registered in the system first.

**Problem:** Appointments not showing
- **Solution:** Check the date filter. Verify you're viewing the correct date range.
