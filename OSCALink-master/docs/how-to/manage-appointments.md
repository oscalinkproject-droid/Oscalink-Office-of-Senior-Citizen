# How to Manage Appointments

Quick steps to create, view, and manage appointments.

## View Appointments

1. Go to **Timeline** from sidebar
2. Select **Calendar** or **List** view
3. Navigate dates using the calendar controls

## Create Appointment

1. Click **"New Appointment"** or **"+"** button
2. Fill in the form:
   - Senior name (search and select)
   - Service type
   - Date and time
   - Notes (optional)
3. Click **"Schedule"**

## Reschedule

1. Click on the appointment
2. Click **"Reschedule"**
3. Select new date/time
4. Confirm

## Cancel

1. Click on the appointment
2. Click **"Cancel"**
3. Provide reason
4. Confirm cancellation

## Check Today's Schedule

The Dashboard displays today's appointments in the "Today's Schedule" widget.
