# How to Export Reports

Quick steps to generate and export reports.

## Prerequisites

- City Administrator role required
- Access to the Reports page

## Steps

1. **Navigate to Reports** from the sidebar
2. **Select report type**:
   - Senior Citizen Summary
   - Assistance Requests
   - Appointments
   - Status Distribution
3. **Set filters** (optional):
   - Date range
   - Barangay/Sector
   - Status
4. **Choose format**:
   - **PDF** — For printing and formal documents
   - **CSV** — For data analysis in Excel
5. **Click "Export"** or "Download"

## Report Types

| Report | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| **Senior Summary** | Total seniors by barangay, age demographics |
| **Assistance Requests** | Requests by type, status, date range |
| **Appointments** | Scheduled vs. completed appointments |
| **Status Distribution** | Active, Inactive, Transferred, Deceased counts |

## Tips

- Use date filters to narrow down results
- Select specific barangays for localized reports
- Export to CSV for further data manipulation
