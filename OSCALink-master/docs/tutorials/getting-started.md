# Getting Started with OSCALink

This tutorial will guide you through logging in and navigating the OSCALink portal for the first time.

## Objectives

By the end of this tutorial, you will be able to:
- Log into OSCALink using your credentials
- Navigate between different pages
- Understand your dashboard

## Step 1: Access the Login Page

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
2. Enter the OSCALink URL provided by your administrator
3. You will see the landing page with the title "Digital Governance for Our Senior Citizens"
4. Click the **"Access Administrative Portal"** button or navigate to `/login`

## Step 2: Enter Your Credentials

1. On the login page, enter your **Email** in the first field
2. Enter your **Password** in the password field
3. Click the **"Sign In"** button

> **Note:** If you don't have credentials, contact your system administrator.

## Step 3: View Your Dashboard

After successful login, you will be redirected to your **Dashboard**. The dashboard shows:

- **Statistics Summary** — Total number of senior citizens, broken down by status
- **Request Queue** — Number of pending assistance requests
- **Today's Schedule** — Upcoming appointments for today
- **Demographics Charts** — Visual representation of senior citizen data by barangay

## Step 4: Navigate the System

Use the **Sidebar** on the left side of the screen to navigate to different sections:

| Page | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Dashboard** | Overview of OSCA operations |
| **Directory** | List of all registered senior citizens |
| **Requests** | Manage assistance requests |
| **Timeline** | View and manage appointments |
| **Reports** | Generate PDF/CSV reports (Admin only) |
| **Settings** | Manage your profile |

## Step 5: Log Out

When you're finished using the system:

1. Click your name or profile icon in the top-right corner
2. Select **"Log Out"** from the dropdown menu
3. You will be returned to the landing page
