# Office of the City Mayor - Office of the Senior Citizen’s Affairs [1]
## External Service (City of Cotabato) [1]

### MANDATES
* **R.A. 9994** or The Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010 [1].
* **Ordinance No. 06, Series of 1994** – An Ordinance amending certain provisions of Ordinance No. 22, Series of 1993, entitled “An Ordinance creating and/or establishing the office for Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) pursuant to R.A.7432, appropriating and for other purposes” [1].
* **Ordinance No. 22, Series of 1993** – An Ordinance Creating and/or Establishing the Office for Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) pursuant to R.A. No. 7432 appropriating funds therefor and for other purposes [1, 2].
* **RESOLUTION NO. 7251, Series of 2023** – A RESOLUTION DECLARING THE EXCLUSIVE USE OF THE MULTI-PURPOSE SENIOR CITIZENS CENTER BUILDING FOR SENIOR-RELATED PROGRAMS AND ACTIVITIES, SPONSOR: HON. KUSIN S. TAHA [2].

### MISSION
An empowered Senior Citizens Organization in partnership with responsive government agencies, civil society group and private sectors to easily access goods and services leading to a graceful and enjoyable aging [2].

### VISION
To plan, implement and monitor the work programs in pursuant to the objectives of RA 9994 [2].

### CORE VALUES (E.Q.U.A.L.I.T.Y) [3]
* **E** – Empowerment of Senior Citizens [3]

### SERVICES AND HOW TO AVAIL THEM (FRONTLINE SERVICE) [3]
The Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) is committed to provide the following major frontline services to help the general public, the private sector, and our client; local government units, agencies, department and offices [3]:

#### A. Issuance of Senior Citizens Identification Card (with Booklets for Medicine/Hospital, Grocery/Agricultural Products, Movies), Cancelation of SC ID and Application of SC Late Birth [4]
* **Office/Division:** Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) Cotabato City Government [4]
* **Classification:** Simple [4]
* **Type of Transaction:** G2C – Government to Transacting Public; G2G – Government to Govt. employee or agency [4]
* **Who may avail:** All Senior Citizens [4]

**Checklist of Requirements:**
* **NEW:** Barangay Certificate (Countersigned by Barangay SC President), Birth Certificate and any Valid ID, CEDULA, Recent 2x2 Photo (1 copy). Secure from Barangay Office, PSA/NSO, Office of the City Treasurer, Legal Office/Firm [4, 5].
* **RENEWAL:** Barangay Certificate (Applying for Renewal, Original, Countersigned), Photocopy of SC ID with 3 signatures, Recent 2x2 Photo (white background), CEDULA [5].
* **LOST:** Affidavit Of Loss, CEDULA, Recent 2x2 Photo (1 copy) [6].
* **CORRECTION:** Senior Citizen ID, Birth Certificate and any valid IDs [6].
* **CANCELLATION OF SC ID:** Senior Citizen ID (Secure from OSCA) [6].
* **APPLICATION FOR SC LATE BIRTH:**
  1. Verification for Records/PSA Negative (PSA) [6, 7]
  2. Verification Fee 100/LCR (Office of the Civil Registrar) [6, 7]
  3. Joint Affidavit of 2 disinterested Persons witnessing the Birth (PAO) [6]
  4. Barangay Certificate with SC President signature (Barangay Office) [6, 8]
  5. OUT OF TOWN Affidavit of Birth [7]
  6. MDR (Philhealth) (PHIC) [7, 8]
  7. Comelec Certificate (Updated) (COMELEC) [7, 8]
  8. CEDULA [7]
  9. National ID (Postal Office) [7, 8]
  10. Baptismal Certificate [7]
  11. Police/NBI Clearance (NBI Office) [7, 8]
  12. PICTURE with latest Calendar [7]
  13. Birth Certificate of Sibling [7]
  14. Marriage Contract [7]
  15. GREEN BIRTH (Barangay) [7]
  16. OSCA ID Xerox with 3 Signatures [7]
  17. OSCA Certificate of SC ID holder with details [7]

**Process:**
| Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees | Processing Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **For new, renewal, lost/correction:** Apply for SC ID | Receive requirements, issue immediately the ID and booklets | None | 3 minutes [8] |
| **Cancellation:** Client submits OSCA ID | Receive requirements, issue Certificate of Cancelation | None | 3 minutes [8, 9] |
| **Late Birth:** Client submits requirements | Receive requirements, Verification/Validation | None | 6 mins. [9] |
| **TOTAL** | | **None** | **12 minutes** [9] |

#### B. Application for Senior Citizens Cash Card ATM Privilege and Social Pension [9]
* **Office/Division:** OSCA Cotabato City Government [9]
* **Classification:** Simple [9]
* **Type of Transaction:** G2C, G2G [9, 10]
* **Who may avail:** All Senior Citizens [10]

**Checklist of Requirements:**
* **CASH CARD:** Photocopy of SC ID, Proof of Billing, Barangay Clearance, COMELEC Certification (at least 2 yrs registered), Application Form for Cash Card ATM. Secure from OSCA/OSWDS/DSWD 12/LANDBANK [10].
* **SOCIAL PENSION:** Three (3) 2x2 pictures, Xerox copy of SC ID (w/ OSCA Head signature), Original Barangay Certificate of Residency, Voters Certificate (Xerox and Original). Secure from OSCA/OSWDS/DSWD 12 [10, 11].

**Process:**
| Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees | Processing Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Apply for Cash Card ATM** | Receive requirements, Verification/Validation, Releasing by schedule thru Landbank branches/offsite | None | 1 month & 3 minutes [11] |
| **Apply for Social Pension** | Receive requirements, Verification/Validation | None | 3 minutes [11] |
| **TOTAL** | | **None** | **1 month and 6 minutes** [12] |

#### C. Application for Senior Citizens Burial Assistance and Data Collection [12]
* **Office/Division:** OSCA Cotabato City Government [12]
* **Classification:** Simple [12]
* **Type of Transaction:** G2C, G2G [12]
* **Who may avail:** All Senior Citizens [12]

**Checklist of Requirements:**
* **BURIAL ASSISTANCE:** Registered Death Certificate, Barangay Certificate of Indigency, Funeral Contract (for Non-Muslims), VIDEO/PHOTO documentations ON CELLPHONE, Deceased & claimant’s valid I.D. Secure from OSCA/OSWDS [12, 13].
* **DATA COLLECTION:** Recent 2x2 pictures (1 copy), Photocopy of SC ID with 3 signatures, Photocopy of BIRTH CERTIFICATE. Secure from OSCA [13].

**Process:**
| Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees | Processing Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Apply for Burial Assistance** | Receive requirement, Verification/Validation, Preparation of financial document | None | 1 week & 3 minutes [13, 14] |
| **Data Collection** | Receive requirement, Interview, Releasing by schedule | None | 1 week & 3 minutes [14] |
| **TOTAL** | | **None** | **2 weeks and 6 minutes** [14] |
